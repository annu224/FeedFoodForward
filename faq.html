FeedFoodForward is an AI-powered food redistribution platform that connects food donors (hotels, restaurants, cafés, bakeries, event kitchens) with verified receivers (ashrams, NGOs, old-age homes, orphanages) through trained delivery drivers. We combine photo-based AI inspection, smart receiver matching, live GPS tracking, and QR-verified handover — all in one place.

Upload a photo of the surplus food. Google Gemini estimates food type, freshness score (0–100), visible spoilage, and pickup urgency. It's a helpful pre-screen — not a safety guarantee. The driver physically inspects the food before pickup and makes the final safety call.
Two QR scans confirm the chain of custody:
  • The driver scans the donor's Pickup QR at collection
  • The Ashram scans the driver's Delivery QR at handover
Both are timestamped and logged. If the wrong QR is scanned, the delivery is blocked.
Click Sign Up → choose Ashram/NGO. Provide your organization name, type, number of residents, address, and food requirements. Once verified, you can browse and claim available donations on the platform.
On your Track Deliveries page, click Scan Delivery QR when the driver arrives. The driver scans the QR and the delivery is confirmed instantly. You can also view a live map with the driver's location and ETA.
Sign up as a Driver and provide your vehicle type, driving licence number, and operating area. Once approved, you'll get access to the Driver Hub where you can accept missions, navigate to pickups, and confirm deliveries via QR scans.
If food fails inspection (smell, appearance, packaging, temperature, or prep-time), tap Reject Food in the Driver Hub. The mission is cancelled and the donor is notified immediately. No penalty is applied — safety always comes first.
