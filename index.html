FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is FeedFoodForward free to use?

Yes. Donating surplus food and requesting food as an Ashram or NGO is completely free. We only ask partners to keep pickup windows accurate so drivers aren't kept waiting.

Who can donate food?

Hotels, restaurants, cafés, bakeries, cloud kitchens, banquet halls, corporate kitchens and event organizers can all donate surplus food.

How does the AI freshness inspection work?

You upload a photo of the surplus food. A vision model estimates food type, freshness score and urgency. The result helps match the donation to the nearest suitable receiver.

How is delivery verified?

Two QR scans confirm the chain of custody: the driver scans the donor's QR at pickup, and the Ashram scans the driver's QR at handover. Each scan is timestamped and logged.
