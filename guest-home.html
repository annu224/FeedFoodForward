How It Works

A simple process. A big impact.

Learn More →
🍱 1

Donate Food

Restaurants and hotels list surplus food with a quick photo and pickup details.

🤖 2

AI Pre-Screening

Our AI checks freshness, food type, and urgency — never a replacement for human inspection.

✅ 3

Pickup Verification

A trained driver inspects the food at pickup and scans the donor's QR to confirm safety.

Track & Deliver

Follow the delivery live on the map. The ashram scans the delivery QR to confirm receipt.
