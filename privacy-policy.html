01

Information We Collect

When you register or use FeedFoodForward, we may collect the following:

  • 👤Account details — name, email address, phone number, and role (donor, ashram, or driver)
  • 🏢Organization info — hotel/restaurant name, ashram name, address, and food requirements
  • 📍Location data — pickup and delivery addresses, plus live GPS during active deliveries
  • 📷Food photos — analysed by Google Gemini solely for AI pre-screening purposes
✓

We never sell your personal data. Any information you share stays within FeedFoodForward's platform.

🎯
02

How We Use Your Information

Every piece of data we collect has a specific, honest purpose:

  • 🎯Matching — connecting donations with the nearest verified receivers
  • 📡Live tracking — letting you follow your delivery on the map in real-time
  • 🔐Verification — QR-code confirmation at pickup and handover
  • 🔔Notifications — pickup reminders, delivery alerts, platform updates
  • 📊Impact reports — CSR statistics and environmental metrics
📍
03

Location & Live Tracking

Drivers share live GPS location only while on an active delivery mission. Donors and receivers can see this location to track their delivery progress on the map.

  • ⏱️Location sharing stops automatically when the mission ends
  • ⚙️You can disable location sharing at any time from Settings → Privacy
  • 🔒Location data is encrypted and never shared with third parties
🔗
04

Third-Party Services

We work with the following trusted services. Each handles your data under its own privacy policy:

  • 🤖Google Gemini — AI image analysis for food inspection
  • 🗺️OpenStreetMap & Nominatim — maps and geocoding
  • 🛣️OSRM — road-accurate route calculation
  • 🗄️MongoDB Atlas — encrypted cloud data storage
🛡️
05

Your Rights

You have full control over your data. From your Settings page you can:

  • 📥Download your data — get a full copy as a JSON file
  • 👁️Control visibility — decide if your profile appears publicly or on the leaderboard
  • 📡Toggle location sharing — enable or disable GPS anytime
  • 🗑️Delete your account — permanently remove all your data
💡

Tip: You can exercise any of these rights instantly — no waiting for approval, no customer service tickets.

⏳
06

Data Retention

We keep your data only as long as necessary:

  • ✅Active accounts — retained for as long as your account is open
  • 📊Donation records — retained for 24 months for CSR reporting purposes
  • 🗑️Deleted accounts — purged permanently within 30 days
