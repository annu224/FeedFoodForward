Safe to Donate

Fresh, properly handled food

✓Freshly cooked or baked — ideally within 4 hours of preparation
✓Properly packaged — clean, sealed, or covered containers
✓Safe temperatures — hot food ≥ 60°C, cold food ≤ 5°C
✓Sealed packaged goods — bakery, dairy, or canned within expiry date
✓Fresh produce — firm fruits and vegetables, no visible blemishes
❌

Never Donate

Unsafe or spoiled food

✕Food left out >2 hours — bacteria grows fast at room temp
✕Visible spoilage — mould, discolouration, or off smell
✕Plate leftovers — food already served to someone
✕Expired or damaged — expired goods, dented or swollen cans
✕Raw meat & seafood — unless cold-chain is guaranteed throughout

Best Practices

Every rescue needs care

A few simple habits make every donation safer and more reliable.

🧊

Cool quickly

Move cooked food into the fridge within 90 minutes of preparation. Bacteria grows fast at room temperature.

🍱

Package smart

Use clean, food-grade containers. Seal properly and label with preparation time and contents.

⏱️

Timing matters

List your donation the moment you know there's surplus. Fast pickups = fresher food for receivers.
