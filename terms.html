Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account on FeedFoodForward, you agree to these terms in full. If you don't agree with any part of it, please don't use the platform.

These terms apply equally to donors (hotels, restaurants, cafés, bakeries, event kitchens), receivers (ashrams, NGOs, shelters, old-age homes), and drivers (delivery partners).

✓

Simple version: Use the platform honestly. Don't abuse it. Treat the people on the other end with dignity.

👤
02

Who Can Use the Platform

You can register on FeedFoodForward if you:

  • ✅Are 18 years or older
  • ✅Represent a legitimate food business, verified ashram/NGO, or have a valid driving licence for delivery partners
  • ✅Provide accurate and truthful information in your signup (name, address, phone, etc.)
  • ✅Agree to keep your account credentials secure and private

Admin accounts cannot be self-registered — they're created only by the platform owner.

🍱
03

Donor Responsibilities

By donating food on FeedFoodForward, you agree that:

  • ✅All donated food is safe, freshly-prepared, and properly stored
  • ✅Food has not been left out for more than 2 hours
  • ✅Quantity, prep time, storage, and pickup address are accurate
  • ✅You will cooperate with the driver during physical inspection at pickup
  • ✅You will never donate spoiled, contaminated, or unsafe food
⚠️

Donors are legally and morally responsible for the food they donate. If the driver rejects food for safety reasons, the delivery is cancelled immediately and logged.

🏛️
04

Receiver Responsibilities

By registering as an Ashram, NGO, or shelter, you agree to:

  • ✅Show the Delivery QR to the driver when food arrives
  • ✅Confirm receipt honestly — no false claims
  • ✅Use donated food only for its intended community purpose
  • ✅Never resell or redirect food for profit
  • ✅Report any food-safety issues through the platform immediately
🚚
05

Driver Responsibilities

As a delivery partner, you agree to:

  • ✅Physically inspect every donation — smell, appearance, packaging, temperature, prep time
  • ✅Scan the Pickup QR at collection and the Delivery QR at handover
  • ✅Reject any food that fails safety standards — no exceptions
  • ✅Maintain a clean, safe vehicle appropriate for food transport
  • ✅Handle food with dignity and care from pickup to delivery
🛡️

Drivers are the final safety checkpoint in the chain. Every rejection is protected — no penalty for refusing unsafe food.

🤖
06

AI Pre-Screening Disclaimer

The AI visual inspection (powered by Google Gemini) is a helpful pre-screening tool only. It is not a food safety guarantee, and it does not replace human verification.

  • 🤖AI estimates freshness, category, and urgency based on a photo
  • 🚫AI can be wrong — it cannot detect all contaminants or odours
  • 🧑The driver's physical inspection at pickup is the final decision
⚠️

FeedFoodForward is not liable for any food-safety outcomes arising from decisions based solely on the AI assessment. Never donate food you wouldn't eat yourself.

⚖️
07

Fair Use Policy

You agree not to:

  • ✕Scrape, copy, or misuse platform data
  • ✕Attempt unauthorised access to other accounts
  • ✕Submit false donations, requests, or issue reports
  • ✕Harass, threaten, or discriminate against other users
  • ✕Use the platform for any illegal or commercial-resale purpose

FeedFoodForward reserves the right to suspend or terminate any account that violates these rules — with or without notice, depending on severity.

08

Payments & Fees

FeedFoodForward is completely free for all users — donors, receivers, and drivers. There are:

  • ✅No commissions on donations
  • ✅No subscriptions or registration fees
  • ✅No hidden charges — ever

If this ever changes for premium features (like advanced analytics), we'll notify existing users 30 days in advance, and the core rescue flow will remain free forever.
