Something not working?

These cover 90% of the issues donors run into.

📷 Image won't upload

Use JPG, PNG, or WEBP under 5 MB. If it still fails, refresh the page or try a different browser.

🚫 "No food detected"

The AI couldn't spot food in your photo. Try better lighting, centre the food, and avoid blurry shots.

🔐 QR code won't scan

Brighten your screen, clean the driver's camera lens, and hold steady about 15 cm away.

📦 Can't see my donations

Check the user chip top-right. If wrong account, log out and log back in. Refresh if needed.

🚚 No driver assigned yet

Matching is automatic and usually takes 5–10 min. Verify your pickup address is correct.

🗺️ Map won't load

Check your internet. Disable ad-blockers temporarily. Try reloading the page.
